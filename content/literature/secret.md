If I had a secret,

I would hide it.

If someone wanted me,

I would say no,

And ask a lot.

I would give myself to those

Who knew the secret.

Or those who gave me a lot,

Would know my secret.

They wouldn't back away,

Would they?

I am like this.

Aren't you too?
