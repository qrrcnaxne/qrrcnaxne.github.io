The wind is gushing\
Past it goes through me

Is it good\
Feels unnatural

Smells of it\
The water stale and blue

Goes in my eyes\
Comes out though my ears

My hair whips\
On my forehead

Like a rain of poison sting\
On the side of an elephant.

He won't be hurt\
But he'll notice

The sound of Piano Brothers\
A Thousand Years

Mixing with the wind\
On my drums

D saddens C\
That's what I learnt

And then cheerfully again\
How music is made.
