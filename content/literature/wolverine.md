My mom says I look like wolverine\
I don't think so\
I make hair like that\
So my sweat dries fast fast

Different hair styles have I tried\
Afro, porcupine, bald and flat\
I end up not liking them\
It fits different for face shapes

I wonder it has to do with self image\
Because people feedback\
I end up not believing them\
They're just saying it for its sake
