There she was. I met her in a car once. I felt bad for not saying good bye when I left. I don't remember when I first met her. She didn't hold it against me. I got attached. This didn't happen so easily. But this time it did. There was an innocence in her. Something that doesn't manifest itself the way it did. A kind of purity. She needed growing up.

But her uncorrupted soul sparked something in me. It reminded me of something that existed in myself. Something that I didn't want to lose but was skipping away from me. Being with her reminded me of that what I was trying hard to hold on it.

Why are you so close to her? Someone had wondered that about us. Not vocally. Our minds existed at the same level. Immaturity and innocence; childlike.
