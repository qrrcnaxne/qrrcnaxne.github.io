What if you had a power?\
One that you were born with.\
One that you didn't know you had.

One day while training,\
You think to yourself.\
Hey let me try this out,\
and it comes out,\
blowing the earth away.

You are strong,\
and you didn't know.\
I wish I knew before,\
Things could've been different

But they couldn't have.\
You weren't born with it,\
it grew in you.

Tragedies, betrayals, misery, despair.\
Things that you cursed,\
Were blessings in disguise.

What now?\
Can I get stronger?\
For that you have to go\
in bigger caves,\
and fight bigger dragons.
