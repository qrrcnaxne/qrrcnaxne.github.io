Be a cleric in training, travelling with an elderly mentor.

Staying in a death themed resort.

Met a childhood familiar person staying at the diagonal room with granny in front of her.

Explore room, find out next door is same room as me. Investigate.

Meet the granny, question her.

Childhood aquantance finds us talking and tries to devourer granny's soul.

Granny revels herself to be a powerful witch who is hunting an evil creature that devours souls of children.

Confrontation with the evil witch, good witch wins, releases all the souls of children that were devoured.

Souls of children thank the granny and narrate their dreams and what they'll do now after they have been released. One will go to sleep and dream of a gentler world. One will travel the cosmos.

Cleric me commorates this happy day as children's day.
