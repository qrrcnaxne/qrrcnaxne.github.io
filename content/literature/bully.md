He was a lonely child growing up. Sure he had "friends", but he never felt included or as a part of their group. He was always an outsider. He had a very twisted sense of self-worth. Perhaps this is why he could never make close friends. Of course this changed later, but that's a story for another day. He always found relationships-of-purpose more natural; his teachers loved him, his parents loved him, he got his complete sense of worth from how much they liked him, and he had developed appropriate tools growing up to get them to like him; self-pity, less confidence disguised as modesty, humbleness; defensiveness, the ability to draw sympathy and pity from others, those were his best friends. Of course it was fucked up, what did you expect?

All this din't work out with people who considered him equal to him; the only people who liked him were the ones who looked down on him. He blamed it all on his "bad social skills". Some of his closer friends were exactly like this too. Toxic people have a way to sniff each other out, it was mysterious. Regular healthy people despised him; but they were cool; so he hung out with them anyway; it was good for his reputation that he had healthy friends; adults liked that; it served his purpose. He had no idea why they let him hang out with them. School had a policy of divide and rule and still he worked hard to maintain his old friends. Most of them din't like him, he din't care; some of them did, he din't care about them either.

Such was his story of his isolation. So when he moved places, he had to make new friends to please the overlords. New friends hated him; he had no sense of how to interact with normal people. He forced himself on them, they started hating him more. Fights started breaking out. He never hit anyone; so effectively, it was bullying. They dragging his shirt and tore it and put mud on his skin. He used to go out everyday, get beaten up, come home, wash up and next day repeat. They kicked him, casually, on his legs, and then he gave up and fell down and then they kicked him on his arms. Eventually they started accepting him; he was fun; he came everyday, got the shit beaten out of him, never complained, and when someone got curious about his wounds he used to lie his ass off. They took his money and ate his snacks without giving him and them bought more snacks form his money and ate those alone too.

His dad knew. He saw it once. He was worried if his little Timmy lost his cool one day, he was gonna kill those bullies; people mistook him for a weakling, but in reality he was quite strong. His dad went to speak with their kids' parents explaining him the situation. They discarded him. His dad tried talking to those kids. They listened initially, but when nothing happened, they continued, this time a little scared. His dad knew talking to timmy was of no use.

The fateful day did come eventually. They were playing with little Timmy; and they decided to take it up a notch. This time the punches were more powerful, targets were his vitals, kicks landed harder and on the mud covered regions, there was a little blood too. It was painful.

"You piece of shit, how dare you complain?"

"But I din't complain"

"You lying fucktard, your dad threatened us"

"I have no idea what you're saying, my dad doesn't know anything," he said it in a squinky voice.

More attacks followed.

"Stop it, I can't take it anymore," squinky continued.

"You fuckin piece of shit, did you think you had a choice? Eat this"

More kicks, more punches, more blood. He was laying down dragging his knees to his chest and hands over his head. He had invented this defense stance on his own to protect his vitals. Tears flowed. He was getting beaten up for things he hadn't done. If his parents knew, there was no point. He decided to give up taking their blows and end it once and for all. These friends had lost their significance if his parents knew. At this point, bullies had reduced their attacking because they were tired, but they were still on it.

He got up and shoved his ankle in the smaller bully's mouth; bully retreated in pain. Then Timmy held the older bully's head and banged it hard on the wall. There was a scream. Younger bully saw this and tried to run away. Timmy held his legs and punched his head hard. The older bully was near the point of unconsciousness. Young bully was trying desperately hard to run away. Little Timmy tried to not let him. Gave him repeated blows to his head. He passed out. Timmy got scared. He turned to the older bully and did the same thing. He sat on his back as he laid down. He had to sit weird because of his leg injuries. Delivered blows after blows to anywhere he could. His neck, his head, all numb at this point. Timmy started crying, crying hard. His vision became fuzzy, his hands of blood, he was screaming. He din't realize that older bully had passed out. He had lost his mind, he continued giving blows to him till his arms gave out, then he gave some more blows. After he was done, he sat there in mud, properly now, blood everywhere, crying hard.

He was crying at his loss. He worked so hard at getting them to like him, now it was over, he suffered so many blows and injuries, they had finally started to like him, now it was over. Fuck.

What were they saying about his dad? How was he going to explain him this? How was he going to explain all of them any of this? Thinking about this made his head heavy. He cried harder, his wounds had gone numb. The only pain he felt was inside. His eyes had recovered a little vision since last time he remembered. But now they were giving up again. His mind decided to join in with his eyes. His other senses followed the lead. He din't know what he was feeling, probably nothing. There was a loud thud on the side of his head; he had a faint taste of mud mixed with a little blood. There was nothing after; he had passed out too.
