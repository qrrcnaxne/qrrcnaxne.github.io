The day was especially bad\
Unquenching lethargy

I tried to read\
Swapped 3 books\
Eyes gorged out.

I tried to play\
Leveled a warlock\
Pvped with a mage\
Fucked up

I saw YouTube\
Teded, writing,\
Couldn’t sit through

I listened to songs\
What did the Fox say?\
Shut the fuck up

I tried watching TV\
Toxic as fuck

Now I’m writing shitty poems about my day\
They’re alright.\
The end of the day

I tried to move\
Couldn’t

I skipped gym\
To pack my room\
Depression filled me up

I tried to chat\
People are shit.

I laid down\
Legs too restless

The day remains\
Ate too much trash in the last 3 days.\
Going again for a trashy feast

Gym instructor tells me to change my workout\
Half the time cardio

I hate cardio\
I’m scared I might lose interest in gym.

Fuckin shit
