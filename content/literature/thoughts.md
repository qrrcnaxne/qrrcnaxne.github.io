Who are you? Who am I?\
Let's find out.

What do you care about? What do I care about?\
What do our lives look like?\
Let's find out.

I don't want to invade. Do I mind exposing?\
I don't know. What is the spirit of trust?

Different people sing differently.\
Some don't like the way we sing.\
They curse us, try to make us sing in their way.\
It used to be like that. No more.

That's the nature of growing up.\
Things were decided when we were little.\
Now we have more freedom to explore each other.\
Now we have more responsibility.\
Awareness eludes us all.\
We all struggle to search.

It's a burden to bear; a load to lift.\
Like all the other burdens,\
different people get different sizes.\
Sometimes it's heavy, and someone can't lift it.\
But sometimes it's light.\
Does the one with light help out\
the ones with heavy?

What is the spirit of trust?\
The light one says,\
"Sure I'll help you,\
but you have to sing my song."

How does that work?\
Is this how it's supposed to be?\
Why even help?\
Because you're an evangelist?

Does help not exist to gain strength?\
Sure it does. That's the good help.

What if the only help you get is evangelical in nature?

How do you run away?\
How do you become independent?\
How do you gain freedom?

What differentiates help from exploit, from manipulation?

Who are you? What is your worth?\
I will help you if I find you worthy,\
if you show me promise.

Promise to sing your song?\
Can't say...
