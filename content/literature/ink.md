I'm sitting in my dentist's office. The shelf near the receptionist's desk is full of specialized dentistry tools. Thin brushes, flosses, thick brushes, no brushes.

This was the end of fifth year I hadn't brushed my teeth. They were mostly fine. Except my gums were non existent. And black. My inner teeth were yellow. They grew yellower the more you looked in. My sides were hurting since some days. I came here because I was annoyed with the pain.

Dentist says I need some pulling out and some root canaling. I had no choice. The drill started. It went in. The pain spread in my head like an ink drop fell in water. I was clutching the handle. They weren't designed for clutching. This pain was something new.

Doctor smiles at me. Tells me to visit again two weeks after. Tells me to eat an ice cream. What's so special about ice cream? I don't want an ice cream. I wanted the pain to go away.

I pay him and begin climbing down the stairs. They were narrow. There was rust. They were dirty. It was dark too. I see light, I look at the world. I had a prescription in my hand. I had to take these medicines so I would heal faster. I searched for my vehicles. I'll stop by the medicine shop which has that good looking girl on the counter. Looking at pretty girls makes me feel good.

That was a bad idea, dentist told me to get the medicines specifically from that one shop near his clinic. They know each other, and he keeps the usual medicines. But I decide to escape the guilt by giving a feast to my eyes.

I'm racing down the lanes in autopilot. Passage goes by and I'm at the pretty girl's shop. I hand her the prescription. I was returned a pretty smile and told to wait.

This is so shallow. What was I expecting? I was still feeling like garbage. It used to work before. It was such a good feeling. I remember. It isn't anymore.

She goes in, does some digging, scavenging, talking; with her father. Her father returned to me with the prescription and told me they din't have it. Curse my luck. I'll have to go back again. I suck it up. Pity myself and begin riding through the passage of time. This time the usual shop. I get the medicines.

I wanted something. The urge. Something to eat, something to drink, something to feel good. I was upset. I can't go home like this. I grow restless. The passage gets harder, roads more crude, drivers more harsh. I have to have something.

I call up my friend asking if he could hang out. He's studying. I decide to go alone. Get a beer in the cheap neon lit bar. Have a meanest extra cheese burger they had. I was alone person there. The staff dint judge me. They were surprisingly cooperative. Gave a flawless service. I smiled. I was out of money to order anything more. I banged a fat note on the reception, and left.

I reach home. Up to my room. On my bed. Take the covers over me. It had been more than six years since this surfaced in me. I couldn't do anything so I won't do anything. But I need to do something. But ended up disappointed always. No work was ever done. There was no reward. I felt like a beast of burden. I was out of motivation. Exhausted. Breathing felt like too much work. I was beating myself up.
