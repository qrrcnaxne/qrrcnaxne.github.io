In a cage I was\
Tied by chains\
Chains thicker than my claws

I growled\
They giggled

Growls louder than the thunder\
They clapped

I’m a predator\
And a mighty one\
A predator larger than the largest

I wanted to escape\
But I couldn’t\
Like my brothers in the wild

It’s hard out there you know\
Said the master

Listen to me\
You won’t make it out there

I was training in the cage\
With restriction of my chains

How to kill 101\
How to find a mate 302

I can’t let you go because you won’t make it\
Said the master

I can’t make it if you won’t let me go\
Said I

He stood there\
I struggled

You’ve changed\
He said\
You used to be a lovely pup

Fuck off\
I told him

You’ve changed\
He sat near the cage

He watched me\
I struggled

He got a new cage\
He tied me with a new chain\
A bigger one this time

I couldn’t kill him\
He was a master

He tightened the knots\
Strengthened the foundations of the cage.

Isolation\
He told me things\
I refused to budge

I don’t trust you\
I told him

But I only want your good\
He said\
I want to help you

But you’re not\
You’re an abuser

What do you know you vile creature\
You’re but a lowly beast

You have no idea what’s good or bad\
You have no idea how the world is out there

You’ll die\
You won’t make it\
Listen to me, obey me

I was but a chained beast\
I refused to budge\
Refused to listen

I sat there\
Day in and day out\
With no instructions from the master

I sat there\
In the cage of my own making\
Tied by the chains in my own mind\
There was no master this time

And so it goes
