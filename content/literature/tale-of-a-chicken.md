What did the chick say to the duck?\
I wanted to roast the ugly mouse and eat it\
but the duck was unfaithful\
The mouse ran away one day\
Although the chick had no such intentions

The chick never spoke again to the duck\
What I said was horrible it said\
The mouse and the chick talk sometime\
Rat was a good animal at heart\
But he refused to ever meet the chick

How could he say such a think\
I loved you\
The chick said sorry\
Silently\
but the mouse never heard such a thing\
There were no confrontations

The chick is a chicken now all grown up\
The mouse is still a mouse\
and he regrets his investment in the chick back in the days\
she cries\
silently in the rain\
Walking on the lonely road\
A shuddering typhoon

The duck was never seen again to anyone\
A bond of a broken friendship\
What does it say?\
Things happen\
Forgive yourself and move on\
Dwelling in the past will poison your mind

What does the chicken's mind says?\
You were small and stupid\
Grow the fuck up\
It's the mouse's fault too\
He never grew up,\
But he was hurt,\
Hurt heals you know\
It's a two sided blot\
It's partly mouse's responsibility too

Maybe the mouse moved on too\
It's with him now\
There's only so much you can do,\
What else can you do?\
Basically nothing

It's a lost cause\
You have to find another mouse\
Better than the old one\
And be careful before you turn him into a burger\
tell no one about it\
It will taste better if you show patience\
I promise you okay?

Okay said the chicken\
I'm all grown up now\
There are more mice in the world\
tastier and more tender mice

Do you really promise?\
Yes I promise
