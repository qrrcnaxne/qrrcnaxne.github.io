There were colorful hexagons. Like the shape of a honeycomb. Orange, white and green and they were rotating. It geometrically doesn’t make sense that they could rotate. Their edges would run into each other. He couldn’t explain how they were rotating. But they were. It felt like it. He thought if he could make a drawing of them. But he wasn’t particularly good at drawing. Even though everyone said he was. He could never do it. As these thoughts were crossing his mind, the picture was blur. It made no sense. Nothing made sense. It was gone. He tried to remake it. But nothing worked. Now it looked like a colorful paper hexagons with lights below it. The lights were rotating. It made sense. The beauty was lost. It was ugly now. The paper was ugly. The lights could start flickering any time. He didn’t feel good. It felt forced. He gave up trying to recreate. He would never experience those hexagons again. There was a void. Something so surreal. Within his grasp. As soon as he tried to reach for it, it was replaced by something ugly and not quite like it.
