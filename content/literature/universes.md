## Continuity conflicts to settle when merging

- **Cameo and alcohol.** Ghoul: he "dint drink alcohol" and orders a cherry coke. Vampires: he goes for beers and is drunk when bitten.
- **Narration.** Vampires slips between "he" and "I" ("introduce myself", "I hold her left breasts"). Bully is third person (Timmy) while the other boy posts are first person.
- **Venice.** In Ghoul she licks Cameo's neck. She could be one of the three women in Vampires, or related to them.
- **Alberto.** He is missing in Ghoul and never appears in Vampires.
- **Order.** Ghoul and Vampires have no stated order. Ghoul stops after Cameo is told to team up with Venice on an assignment; the bar scene could come before or after it.
- **Duplicated text.** Cave and Object 2 share whole sentences; combining them means picking one wording.
