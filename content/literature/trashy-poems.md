The joy of writing trashy poems

My friends tells me I think too much\
Poem is limiting

I said to him I need infinite words to express myself\
Poem is restricting

He says it's freedom\
I couldn't believe my ears.

I understand shit about poems\
I can't understand any

Writing one was far far away\
Then he shows me his

Poems aren't about beauty or rhyming\
Poems are just a part of honest expression

You can chose your values.\
No one needs to be the next Lord Byron.

Next Bukowski would be just as good.\
Brutally honest vs conventional beauty

I chose the former

So now I make trashy poems\
Which aren't good\
Nor do they rhyme.

So now I finally understand\
The joy of writing trashy poems\
And the joy of reading those too.
