The wind was choreographing the dancing green grass. Flowers joined in. Symphonies of the wind resonated with their dance. The elegance. I was standing on the road. I decide to join in. My shirt dancing against my body, my hair against the wind itself. The first step I took followed the lead of the winds. The wind, grass and all of nature was much experienced than me. Coldness of the sun-filled air felt against my nostrils, a warm feeling yet a cold breeze. Brimmed my trachea. The walk was a long and a hard one. I had to wait every once in a while to drink water. The map showed there was a stream nearby. Maybe I could refill my water bottle. Soon the grassland changed into dense evergreen. Trees stood tall like an army of giants. There was no wind there. No dance. I could hear the bustling and flickering of the leaves on the top of the trees. It was a failed attempt to keep the party going on. There was something foul here. Nature knew it. Animals roamed about trying to warn me. My heart grew restless, my fingers grew cold and my breaths heavier. The forest smelled dusty. It was old. The trees were old and wise. Dried leaves were crumbling beneath my weight. A sense of guilt overcame me. I was waking the trees up from their slumber. I stopped to look around. Nothing had changed. Same old and tall trees, same rusting of leaves, same restless heart. I gulped down a huge mouthful of water. The sense of dread hadn't left me. I wasn't trembling. Yet the feeling was very alive. I opened my bag and gave a quick scan on the map. Traced where I had come to. Water was very important. I had consumed a lot, I needed more. I quick scanned my compass and started walking.

The stream was a short walk far. But it wasn't a stream at all. I came face to face a huge cliff. With water gushing down into a lake.

There was the entrance to the cave. I was standing at it's mouth. The cave looked like an open mouth of a large beast. The lips glittered dark Grey. Inside wasn't pitch dark.

There were ponds, emerald weed, mini cliffs.

I kept losing the way.
