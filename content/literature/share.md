The flower was blooming\
But it stopped;\
Why did it stop\
Where was its food?

There was no food.\
The flower gave it away\
To whom? said he\
To another flower\
So it would bloom and shine in the sun

Why? The prince asked\
Because that was the nature\
Was the other flower a parasite\
No! Quite a opposite.\
That flower had enough food of itself

It pulled away\
It got angry\
It wanted to stop

Okay, said its friend\
Let's stop

The sun set\
The sky grew darker\
The moon started worrying\
A blooming big moon\
All the flowers stopped shining\
They went to sleep

It was another day now.\
I'll give you my food too\
It smiled\
Okay said she\
Let's help each other bloom

It was a new day again\
Flowers were happy\
They ate their own food\
Gave the rest to each other

Yes, said the prince\
That's how it's supposed to be\
The grass started dancing\
The moon smiled
