There were a lot of airplanes flying around today. Only one of them was interesting.\
There was an airplane flying high in the sky. I could hear the sound it was making. The airplane was traveling from left to right. Not as high as I thought it was. But it was high enough to fascinate me. An airforce fighter plane might fly higher.\
It was flying from left to right. It had lights on that I dint see before. I was busy doing something else. There was that sound though. Every time there was sound, I looked up to see if there was any airplane. Not every time, just sometimes. Old habits from second standard.

Seeing an airplane was a subject of great fascination back then. Everyone flocked. It was beautiful and innocent. I lost a tazzo once; because I wanted to see an airplane. Everyone flocked. Everyone stomped. Everyone pushed each other. It was a flocking competition, but everyone learnt to coexist once we reached there. The sounds of children were higher than the airplane sound. Our teachers were joyed too; probably after seeing the children joyed. That's why they took up this job. Everyone was excited. I was too. Because everyone else was. Everyone else was, because every other else was. I wonder if anyone would've been excited if they were alone. I'm sure they would've. It was the cool thing to do. And it fell from my pocket.

I remember I kept it in in the bus. I opened a cheetose pack. They gave a freebie with it. I found a really rare one. It was a gem for me. And for all the others too. I showed it off with great joy. Everyone else was envious. I felt special. This was my time. No one could take it away from me. I sat down on my seat, smiling. To enjoy the moment that was given to me.

But it was gone now. It was lost. It fell down from my pocket. Or someone stole it. I don't think second graders have competent pick pocketing skills.

Teacher told me to search for it. I did, I did again, but I din't find it. Someone else probably found it. And kept it. It was so precious to me. It was precious to everyone else. I was lucky that found it. Unlucky that I lost it.

There was no way I was going to get it back. No amount of money could buy it back. Of course I could buy out the entire industry with enough money. But it won't be the same. I could find out who stole it. Strip search all the kids. But that would be unethical. No one would agree to it. My precious was lost. Forever. I had to make peace with it. And I knew it. Some things are just so precious. I knew nothing else could give me back that something I just lost. It wasn't just a tazzo, it was that thing I felt. It was rare and precious too.\
Was it worth it? Losing something so precious and in exchange, seeing an aeroplane? I convinced myself it was. That was the only way I could keep on believing that life and everything was so fair. That everything happened for a good reason. Seeing an airplane suddenly became more precious to me than the gem. I search for an aeroplane occasionally when it makes that sound. Trying to fill that hole the tazzo left in.

Today, the aeroplane was going from left to right. High up in the sky. Not as high as the air force planes. It made a sound. The sound comes from left when the plane is on left. The sound comes from left when the place is in the middle. The sound comes from the middle when the plane goes to the right. The sound comes from right when plane goes further right.

Was I going insane? Were my ears going bad? Was my hunter brain deceiving me? What the fuck was happening? Was there an invincible airplane tailing the airplane that I was looking at?

Fuckin speed of sound was too slow. Yeah that's it. I'm not delusional. Physics is a mean bitch. Why can't sound travel faster? Why isn't air more dense? Things would go pretty south? I guess it's good the way it is.

This somehow made me supremely uncomfortable. I wonder why?
