Utopia doesn’t exist. Just like alchemy and perpetual motion. What they’re doing, is making people into mindless robots. They take circuits out of people’s brain that makes it possible to think for themselves. Their world is like the one that Orwell depicted in his novel. I’m sure you realize that there are plenty of people looking exactly for that kind of brain death. It makes life a lot easier. You don’t have to think about the difficult things. Just shut up and do what your superiors tell you to do.

-Murakami
