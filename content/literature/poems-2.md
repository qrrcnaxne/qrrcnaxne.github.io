I know I write horrible poems\
But please bear with me

I'm only starting out\
I'll get better after writing about ten thousand

Bukowski motivated me into this\
His poems are shitty\
But they make the job seem easy

So copying him I start out\
Imitation my weapon of choice

I hope I'll get better\
Or it'll all suck big time
