Identity is a bug in human brain. Everything about us is very abstract. Abstract reasoning, abstract maths, abstract fear; that's why we're scared of dark, or fictional aliens. We were evolved to fear predators, brains made it abstract. Identity is essentially that. We can see ourselves. Something makes us similar as well as unique compared to other humans. We abstract it. Philosophers have pondered on this for centuries. It first started with the Ship of Theseus.

Some months ago I wrote a post here titled identity. I've deleted it because it was too small and incomplete and I couldn't say what I wanted to say. I wish I hadn't deleted it. At the end I took it too far and had an epiphany. And I dint understand the meaning of that epiphany. I asked why do we need an identity? I came to a conclusion we don't. Back then I had no sense of what identity actually meant. I've read a lot about it after that. Now I know, more like I've figured it out.

I feel incomplete. Like I'd rather be someone else or someone else. I don't feel I belong where I am, in my body,  in this country, how I am. A better term for this would be Dysphoria. I don't know if this is discomfort. Just an unnatural feeling. Of not being who I am, wanna be, could be. Not feeling myself. Disconnected from myself.

Les Matheson of Teja writes about identity. He's jumbled it up though. Last time I had a conversation with her, we din't end up on the same page. So we stopped. Maybe some time later I'll bring it up.

We all have an identity of ourselves. It's not in our control from the start. Then we get to know who we are. What our beliefs and values are. We find out why we have those particular values and beliefs. That leads us straight to the den of identity. All people are bothered by one of these questions: Who do I want to be? Who am I? What could I be?

Enter Buddhism. The enlightened dude with yellow light behind head. He says, let go. You don't need that. Give up trying to find out who I am, what could I be, what I wanna be. Loss of identity is no loss at all. Identity is an illusion.  The same way you convince yourself that there is no ghost behind the curtain or monster under the bed, there is no identity behind that exterior of yours. Depressing, yeah I know.

People make countless attempts to define who you are. You are your memories, you are your ethics. Without a sense of identity, I'd lose it. But all those things can exist without attaching them to a conjured up image of yourself. You can still chose to relish your memories. You can still chose to be a good person.

Personally I feel, subject of identity is like that of a God. Choosing to believe it gives meaning to some people. It is capable of doing something, sometimes good, many times bad. Can we do without? Yes. I still have an Identity. I'm trying to get rid of it. That's why the dysphoria.

It's liberating. You can safely let it go. It won't leave a hole behind. You won't need to fill a hole. It lifts a load off of you, you feel lighter.
