Hold my hand.\
Teach me to walk.

Pull me up when I trip.\
Hold me back when I run.\
Push me ahead when I stop.

Kiss my wound when I fall down.\
Wipe my tears.\
Hug me like a warm bear.

Tell me it’ll be okay.\
As long as you are there.
