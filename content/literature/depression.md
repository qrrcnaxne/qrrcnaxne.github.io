Drowning in something.\
I don’t know what.

When the density is high.\
When the load is high.\
It slips down.\
To where?\
Bottom of the ocean.

There are feelings.\
Stuff keeps reminding me of them.

We don’t live in a perfect world.\
Ideal is just an idea.\
All those imperfections.\
Slowly chip you away.\
Along with your own imperfections.

How to live life but in this futility?\
Clinging desperately to some hope.\
To a hope of trying to rise up in a world that corrodes you.

How fast can you push?\
How far can you push?\
How far can anyone go?

“Pretty far,” as we know it.\
Despite the futility?

You are going to die.\
Everyone is.\
That is the law of nature.\
One of them that binds us as a part of it.

Despite that.\
How to push?\
Why to push?

An inevitability starts sinking.\
Flora and fauna of the deep ocean.\
Whales, squids, jelly fishes.\
Otherworldly creatures.\
Living in the depths of mother earth.

Deeper you go, darker it becomes.\
Life becomes sparser.\
Drag pulls you down.\
Until you hit the floor.

Bottom of the ocean is a beautiful place.\
There’s no light.\
There is no warmth.

You are left alone.\
Left to fight with yourself.\
Rip yourself apart.\
There’s no one to watch.

Let all the filth out.\
Watch as the things crawl away.\
Watch as the sludge settles down.

And while you sit there,\
watching yourself,\
swallowed in a quiet darkness.

Something swirls.\
Something twirls.\
Something flickers.\
Crawling out of you.

Holds your hands.\
Gives you light.\
Gives you warmth.\
And you chase it.

Shows you out.\
In the sun.\
On the land.
