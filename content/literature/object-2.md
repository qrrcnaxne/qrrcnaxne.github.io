I couldn’t go further. It was far inside. The light guarding it was too bright. I tried to travel around, tried to find other ways to get there. It didn’t work. If I pushed myself hard, I could move. But there was a strange feeling inside. There were things far greater and wiser than I realize. I wanted to get in no matter what. The progress I made was very impressive. But I couldn’t move forward any more than this. I should camp here. Try to get used to the brightness. Let the brightness get used to me. Soak in it, soak it in.

It was an entrance to a cave. The surrounding was beautiful. Fresh green grass and bright magenta flowers sparkled about. Everyone loved the island. No one knew about the cave though. I was standing at its mouth. The cave appeared like an open mouth of a large toothless beast. The stones of its lips glittered dark gray. Inside wasn’t pitch dark. There was a ray. I was told I would be made aware of things as I needed them. I was told to be patient. I wasn’t. I hurried. I never understood the reality of things. I fell down. Ever since this search began, everything had seemed mysterious. They said it wasn’t mysterious at all, I just wasn’t used to it.

There were ponds, emerald weed, mini cliffs. I knew my path perfectly. I had spent a whole year learning how to read maps and reading cave maps. The map was just a tattered cloth. They used to tell me just having knowledge wasn’t enough. I had to go experience it myself to completely understand it. I couldn’t get what I wanted if I didn’t put in the actual work.

So there I was trying to break in to get the object. The object chose its seekers. The object had to test me before it let itself be found.

It was questioning its decision. I wasn’t suited for the job. It was giving mixed signal. Or I was searching for positive signals where there were only negative. I could drop it and go back to where I came from. I was perfectly happy back there. They said the object was capable to either give you great power, or entirely drain your life. You had to find the correct one. A wrong object could send you signals too. I understood very little about them in spite of studying so much.

I thought the object was special. I wasn’t sure. But I was sure. I couldn’t trust my own judgement any more. If the object could simply reject me if it decided. The signals were hurting me. Perhaps I didn’t really need the object. I’ll pause my search. And observe the change in signals. My mind wasn’t perfectly clear. It was painful. I had been trying. The search was futile since I found the cave. It didn’t allow me in. I did go in. I kept losing the way. Light pushed me back with each step I took. Glow was a faint yellow one. It was just a ray or a weapon trying to control me? “Come to me.” “Go away.”

My heart was pounding as if it wanted more room to beat. It wanted to get out but it wouldn’t, if I let it out. I wasn’t following what I was taught to follow. It couldn’t work. I needed the power to claim the object. It pushed when I went close. It pulled when I went far. My life had turned miserable since I decided to come here. I don’t need this. This is hard work. I was perfectly happy being a plebian. This wasn’t making me powerful. I was miserable. I was considering going back. But that was hard too since I had come this far. Would I regret?
